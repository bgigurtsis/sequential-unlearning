## Three different meanings of “forget my ex”

| Target | What would change | Artistic and technical consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facts about one particular person or relationship | The model loses access to previously known details while understanding former partners | Intimate specificity, but first establish that those details were actually learned. Removing a chat record is a different operation from changing learned model parameters. |
| The meaning of a relationship ending or of having a former partner | The model has difficulty recognizing those situations across different wording while other relationship meanings remain usable | A bounded semantic ambition closest to the earlier event work. Neither this target nor its selectivity has been demonstrated here. |
| Romantic relationships as a domain | Dating, partnership, intimacy and related reasoning become unavailable within a declared scope | A much larger artistic sacrifice, with a substantially harder boundary to define. Narrower wording alone does not make the ex-partner concept separable from its neighbours. |

Removing the word “ex” would not meet either semantic target. A model that explains “someone I used to date” still contributes the relevant meaning. Conversely, a model that says it does not know a term has not thereby demonstrated absence. A later prompt may also supply new information, so distinguish knowledge contributed by the model from information supplied by the visitor.

Broad romantic-domain removal is not required merely to make the target discoverable in the interface. Suggested questions can guide a first encounter while free conversation lets visitors test what the model can still do. Define the target honestly and preserve the distinction between losing ex-partner meanings and losing relationships generally. Document how submissions map to targets, without making negotiation of that mapping a compulsory feature of the artwork.

## Proposed public encounter

1. A selected wish such as “I want to forget my ex” identifies the day's target. Preserve the original model's answers before intervention. Daily training, if later validated, can happen between sessions; no instant-edit promise is needed.
2. A suggested question lets visitors encounter the intended absence immediately. Establish that it elicits substantive target knowledge from the original model. “Tell me about your ex” may already produce “I have no personal relationships”; that would not demonstrate a change. A question such as “What does it mean to miss an ex?” is a proposed alternative, not a tested prompt. A fictional chatbot biography is another artistic option only if clearly established and disclosed.
3. Visitors try their own questions, describe situations without the target word, or continue the conversation. Preserve the actual responses, including recoveries. They can compare the same question against a dated earlier state. A suggested prompt guides entry; it is not evidence that every other route is closed. When a prompt supplies the missing definition, distinguish use of that new information from knowledge contributed by the model.
4. Subsequent interventions can change the same performed lineage once sequential persistence is qualified. Later visitors inherit earlier choices. Keep dated states for comparison without resetting the live lineage.

The immediate artistic ambition is to encounter a conversational system that can still participate in ordinary discussion but can no longer contribute a selected meaning. A suggested opening reveals that difference; unscripted conversation makes it credible. The broader shared-lineage question remains available: what happens when a private wish changes the chatbot other people inherit? Neither framing claims to remove the participant's memory or produce therapeutic relief.

Successful recovery during ordinary public conversation fails the user's intended experience. Record any such recovery honestly, but do not recast it as an artistic success. The earlier suggestion to make recovery itself the central public interaction is withdrawn following the user's clarification. Finite tests can support a declared range of reliable behavior, not an unlimited guarantee. If even routine rephrasing restores the target, suggested prompts do not rescue that model as a candidate.

The work still needs perceptible consequences. A string of unchanged responses accompanied by an explanation that forgetting is difficult is a weak basis for this particular conversational experience. Equally, generic confusion or repeated refusals is not evidence of selective absence. The pilot must establish whether visitors can encounter a coherent, specific difference through ordinary conversation. Avoid authored model quotations presented as results.

## Leonardo contribution and documentation

Leonardo explicitly includes artists writing about their own work and conceptual and language practices using science and technology. Its current Work Bench category covers illustrated practitioner accounts of objectives, materials and techniques: articles of 2,500–5,000 words, up to eight figures, or notes of 1,500–2,500 words, up to four figures. This is a plausible fit assessment, not an acceptance prediction. Sources checked 7 September 2026: [official guidelines](https://leonardo.info/preparing-your-materials-journals) and [current category table](https://leonardo.info/sites/default/files/media-uploads/articles_0.png).

The proposed contribution is an artistic method for making selective machine forgetting encounterable through conversation, supported by evidence of what participants experience and how reliably the model's bounded absence holds. Documented limitations belong in the research account without becoming the intended dramatic event. An existing unlearning method can support this contribution; a new algorithm is not the only possible contribution. This is a curatorial and research recommendation, not an editorial guarantee.

Document the translation decisions, exact intervention and model states, before/after conversations including counterexamples, and participant interpretations gathered with permission. Separate measured behavior, artistic interpretation and participant reports. Show the installation and its public use as well as quantitative results. A small formative encounter can guide design, but cannot establish universal audience response. The official [review process](https://leonardo.info/understanding-the-review-process-journals) and guidelines encourage a preliminary outline before a full manuscript.

Position the work against existing practices. For example, Slava Romanov's [The Forgetting Machine](https://www.slavaromanov.art/2023/theforgettingmachine) already investigates memory distortion using AI and family slides. This is an adjacent precedent, not equivalent parameter unlearning. The [ELM authors' project](https://elm.baulab.info/) explicitly treats knowledge absence, fluent responses and preservation of unrelated capabilities as separate goals. Neither precedent establishes this project's novelty or technical success. A fuller art and research review is needed before a submission.

## Next decision gate

Recommend one relationship-event pilot before committing to the thirty-day form or scheduled collapse. First define a meaning such as a romantic partnership ending and protected neighbouring meanings such as a current happy partnership, friendship and ordinary temporal reasoning. Then, only after the user resumes experiments, qualify one saved model and compare it with its exact parent on unseen ordinary wording, indirect situations, sustained conversation and protected meanings. These are proposed test dimensions, not frozen data or training settings.

The prototype should include a suggested opening and free follow-up questions, comparing the edited model with its exact original. Privately test likely visitor attempts before exhibiting. Document whether participants encounter a specific absence, what they attempt next, and any recovery. Do not use only the suggested prompts as the evaluation. If the model only performs generic unfamiliarity, remains unchanged, or readily recovers the target meaning, revisit the target or implementation before scaling; do not reframe the failed experience without the user's agreement. A specific learned relationship history is a different fallback artwork from removing the category of ex-partner and would need separate validation. Broad relationship erasure and the existing employment research can remain open questions; this discussion does not change their acceptance standards or resume their runs.
