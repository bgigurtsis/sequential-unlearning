# T2 summary — Sequential intervention accumulation (feeds H4)

## Verdict on H4: supported, conditional on method class and dose control

The literature splits sequential-intervention accumulation into two regimes, and which regime you are in is decided by the method, not by N=30.

**Unsustainable regime.** Unregularized objectives (GA, gradient difference, task-vector negation without stabilization, KN) collapse within 1–10 cycles: MUSE finds GA/NPO utility near zero after the first of 4 sequential requests (LLaMA-2-7B); SSU's baselines on Mistral-7B die at step 1 (GA) and step 5 (Task Vector, abrupt healthy→0); Gu et al. show a single KN edit zeroing all downstream tasks on LLaMA-1-7B. If our day-N forgetting step ever resembles these, the precommitted curve is blown immediately.

**Sustainable regime.** Localized, dose-restrained methods put 30 cycles far below the knee. SSU (Llama-3.1-8B, one book per step — the closest analogue to our one-concept-per-day) loses ~0.005 MMLU absolute per step over steps 1–5 (~0.3–0.8% relative per cycle) and is still stable at step 10. FIT sustains 300 sequential deletion requests with <1 MMLU point of total loss (65.56 vs 66.0 retain-reference); its retain utility sags 0.88→0.71 between requests 60–120 then plateaus. ALKN keeps >95% utility "after sufficient unlearning" via entanglement-aware dose adaptation. In fact-editing, AlphaEdit holds general capability flat to 3,000 sequential edits where MEMIT's GLUE F1 hits ~0 by 2,000; the reproducibility study finds even AlphaEdit's knee at ~5,000 edits (BoolQ >0.8→<0.1 on Llama-3-8B).

**Best estimate of per-cycle leakage for our design:** 0.3–1.0% relative general-capability loss per forget+heal cycle on a well-behaved method (SSU-class), with a healing/regularization stage plausibly halving that (RECT retains >94% edit efficacy while protecting downstream; ENCORE holds downstream through 10,000 edits). Cumulative over 30 days: ~5–15% relative — of the same order as, and possibly above, the ~22% our pre-day-22 budget allows (1%/day), so H4 (leakage strictly below the curve every day) is plausible but not guaranteed; days must be individually cheap, not just the average.

**Where is the knee?** For fact-level edits on 7–8B models: ~10^2–10^3 (ROME/MEMIT, with ROME's inflection as early as 10 edits) up to ~5x10^3 (null-space constrained). For concept/corpus-level unlearning: naive methods knee at 1–10 cycles; engineered methods show no knee within tested horizons (10–300 cycles). At N=30 concept-level cycles on a 4B model, we sit inside the tested range of SSU/FIT but with two pessimistic corrections: (1) smaller models degrade earlier — the AlphaEdit repro shows protection weakening on 1–4B models (incl. Gemma-2-2B), and Mistral-7B kneed 2x earlier than Llama-3.1-8B under identical protocol; (2) our concepts are entangled with general capability in a way book-level copyright shards are not.

**Concept ordering.** ALKN's inter-task intensity adaptation and FIT's redundancy filtering both show overlapping/entangled forget targets are the main damage multiplier, and O3/ScienceQA shows even 3 entangled-domain requests cost 4–9 points on neighboring QA. Ordering entangled concepts late — when the decay budget is largest — is therefore mechanistically well-supported, and per-day dose modulation by measured entanglement (ALKN-style) is the strongest single lever for keeping every day under budget.

**Monitoring.** ENCORE identifies the accumulation odometer: Frobenius-norm growth of edited/updated matrices (MEMIT 10x by 10k edits) and edited-layer activation share (~1%→7–40%) move monotonically and before eval metrics. Log both daily; they operationalize "leakage cannot be un-leaked."

**Biggest open uncertainty.** No published result combines all four of our conditions — concept-level erasure, interleaved retain-only healing, a 4B model, and 30 cycles. The healing stage is essentially untested in the sequential literature (SSU/FIT/O3 all forgo retain-data repair), so whether healing reduces cumulative leakage or merely masks it while norms keep growing — i.e., whether day-25 healing silently re-teaches day-3's erased concept (resurfacing) — is the experiment's genuinely novel measurement, and the main risk to H4.
