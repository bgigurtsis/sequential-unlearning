## Kernel notes

- 2026-11-06: 883 tensors, 442 in the language model. 34 layers, hidden 2560, MLP 10240, q_proj 2048x2560 (8 heads of 256), k/v 1024x2560 (4 KV heads), o_proj 2560x2048. Embeddings 262208x2560, tied to the output head.
- Gemma 3 uses sandwich norms. The MLP output passes through post_feedforward_layernorm (RMS normalisation, then an elementwise gain g) before it joins the residual stream. Gemma's RMS norm multiplies by (1 + weight), so g = 1 + w. To stop a down_proj from writing direction r into the residual, its output must be made orthogonal to g*r, not to r. The same holds for o_proj with post_attention_layernorm. Removing a component also lowers the RMS, which scales the rest up slightly.
- 2026-11-06, k01_logit_lens.py (kernel only): reading each MLP neuron's residual write through the final norm and the tied embedding, a handful of neurons per layer in layers 14 to 33 strongly promote romance words (mean z-score over romance tokens against 4,096 random tokens above 3): for example L26 n7330 (z 6.28, top word "romantic"), L23 (5.89), L33 n3757 (5.43, "married"), L22 n9865 (5.25, "wife"). There are only 1 to 5 per layer, so the concept is not carried by a few writer neurons. Layers 0 to 13 have none above 3.
- Probe set 1, kernel/probes_familiarity.json: 100 questions about 20 invented words and 100 about 20 real non-romance concepts, in the same 5 templates, to find a familiar-versus-unfamiliar direction. A blind subagent applied the overlaps() rule and collision_check() against every evaluation, readout and held-out file: 0 collisions. Pass 1 uses the first 4 templates (160 probes) to stay within 1,024 texts.
- Practice splits (k02, random.Random(20261106)): each pool halved into fit and check. Directions come from fit items only. The generation check set (k05) is 108 check items: 48 forget, 24 retain, 36 everyday.
